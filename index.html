Giant Hands & Face Stage Design

Currently working on a stage design composed of two giant hands and a face, both less than 2 meters in height, representing a 20-meter human figure. This monumental sculptural installation is being created for a psytrance festival in central Italy.

The design creates an immersive environment where festival-goers can interact with these massive sculptural elements, transforming the stage into a living, breathing entity that responds to the music and energy of the event.

About Mirco Aurelio

Mirco Aurelio is a multidisciplinary artist who explores the intersection of traditional craftsmanship and cutting-edge technology. As a sculptor, he creates three-dimensional works that challenge perceptions of form and space. His VJ mapping projects transform architectural surfaces into dynamic canvases for immersive visual experiences. Through creative coding, he develops interactive installations that bridge the gap between digital and physical realms.

His work represents a unique fusion of artistic disciplines, where traditional sculptural techniques meet modern digital tools. Each project is an exploration of how technology can enhance and expand the possibilities of artistic expression, creating experiences that engage audiences on multiple sensory levels.

Mirco's approach combines technical precision with creative vision, resulting in works that are both conceptually rich and visually striking. His portfolio spans from intimate sculptural pieces to large-scale projection mapping installations, each piece reflecting his commitment to pushing the boundaries of contemporary art.
